# Third-party origin and attribution

EchoCase source is original implementation under the repository's Apache-2.0 license. Architecture was studied from [Jason Taylor CleanArchitecture](https://github.com/jasontaylordev/CleanArchitecture), commit e1ab4cc4d5d7b4e25e57bd8b0514b766573b8c04 (MIT). No Todo, Identity, mediator behaviour, or event-interceptor source was copied. Similar layer names express a shared architectural pattern, not a claim of authorship over that pattern.

The publishing method was studied from [DevOps Delivery](https://github.com/miku-wwl/devops-delivery). The EchoCase publisher and QA tools are independently written. Its evidence, screenshots, performance results and chapter text are not reused.

Official shadcn/ui registry components are generated into frontend/src/components/ui and retain their upstream MIT provenance: https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/blob/main/LICENSE.md . React, TanStack Query, React Flow, Lucide, Vite, Tailwind, Playwright, EF Core, Npgsql and NUnit retain their respective upstream licenses. Package lock files identify installed versions. No images from third-party media are used. All investigation names and incidents are fictional.
